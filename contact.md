I engage selectively, typically in situations where the stakes are high enough that diagnostic judgment and decision quality matter more than additional analysis.

---

## Situations Where I Engage

**Performance under pressure.** A major programme is failing. Capital is at risk. The board needs an independent operator who can diagnose the real constraint and make irreversible decisions — not another review.

**Strategy-execution gap.** The strategy is sound but nothing is moving. Multiple units, regions, or functions are producing coordination costs instead of outcomes. The operating model needs structural redesign.

**Growth that isn't compounding.** Innovation is active but not converting to economic value. Vendor relationships are uncontested. Capital is deployed linearly when it should be deployed asymmetrically. The organization needs a growth engine, not a growth initiative.

**Founder-led scaling.** A CEO or founder needs a peer-level sounding board — someone who improves decision quality without creating a second power centre. Governance adapted to the founder's operating style, not imposed from a corporate template.

---

## How I Typically Engage

**Rapid diagnostics:** 2–4 weeks. Structured stakeholder interviews, fact-base construction, root-cause reframing. Deliverable: a diagnostic that names the real constraint and proposes the intervention architecture.

**Control interventions:** 3–6 months. Governance reset, scope discipline, architectural correction. Typically in crisis or near-crisis situations.

**Coherence programmes:** 6–12 months. Operating model design, strategic alignment, decision architecture redesign. Multi-phase, often involving team assembly.

**Compounding engines:** 12–18 months. Innovation operating model design, vendor leverage, capital discipline institutionalization. The longest engagements, because building repeatable systems takes time.

---

## Confidentiality

All engagements are conducted under strict confidentiality. The case studies on this site are anonymized — client names, identifying details, and sensitive commercial information are removed. Specific case details are shared only in direct, confidential conversation.
